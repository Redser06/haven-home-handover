# ADR-003: Data Retention and Destruction Design

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Haven enforces strict expiry (H-5). The channel between seller and buyer has a defined end of life. After expiry, some data (manuals, device inventory) persists for the buyer, while other data (chat, key safe, tokens) must be permanently destroyed.

## Decision
We will implement a scheduled destruction job that runs server-side to enforce the retention schedule (N-4):
- **Content** (docs, guides): re-parented to buyer, kept indefinitely.
- **Chat**: destroyed at archive + 7-day grace.
- **Key safe secrets**: destroyed immediately at archive.
- **Tokens**: revoked at archive.
- The job must be idempotent (T-13).

## Rationale
- Server-side enforcement (H-4a) guarantees compliance even if users never open the app again.
- Hard deletion of sensitive data minimizes liability and adheres to GDPR data minimization principles.

## Consequences
- Requires a reliable cron or scheduled function mechanism in Supabase.
- The destruction job logic must be heavily tested (T-13) to ensure it never deletes surviving content by mistake.
- Audit events will track deletions for 12 months.
